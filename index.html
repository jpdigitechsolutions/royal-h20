BRAND ESSENCE

Where Hydration Meets Royal Hospitality

Royal H2O was founded on a simple yet elevated premise: hydration at milestone events should be as distinguished and memorable as the celebration itself. A generic bottle on an opulent table diminishes the experience; a bespoke monogram bottle transforms it into a keepsake.

From weddings and anniversary dinners to executive conferences and luxury gifting suites, our bottles feature custom metallic gold-foil labels, bespoke typography, and pristine alkaline water treated with multi-stage reverse osmosis.

Gold-Foil Crests

Custom monograms, couple crests, and brand emblems crafted with reflective metallic foil.

Diamond Filtration

Multi-stage purification paired with essential remineralization for an exceptionally smooth finish.

Bespoke Batches

Flexible batch sizes prepared and scheduled precisely to match your event delivery timeline.

Concierge Service

Direct WhatsApp coordination from initial digital proof approval to manor dispatch.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers regarding customization specifications, design proof turnaround, minimum orders, and dispatch arrangements.

You begin by choosing your bottle style, label finish, and providing your monogram or logo. Our design concierge prepares a high-resolution digital proof for your approval via WhatsApp. Once confirmed, we bottle, label, quality-check, and deliver your batch on your designated date.

We specialize in metallic gold-foil stamping on obsidian black, champagne gold on ivory, warm rose-gold luxe, and modern platinum silver. Embossed crests and matte textures can also be coordinated.

We accommodate intimate occasions starting from 50 bottles up to large-scale galas and weddings requiring thousands of units. Please message our concierge for tailored tier recommendations.

Yes, absolutely. You can send vector files (.AI, .EPS, .SVG, or high-res PNG/PDF) directly via WhatsApp or email. Our team will optimize the artwork for gold-foil plate stamping.

We recommend placing wedding and corporate orders at least 1 to 2 weeks in advance to allow adequate time for artwork proofing, plate preparation, and scheduled delivery. Urgent rush orders may be accommodated based on batch availability.

Our dispatch center is based in Moi, Pune, Maharashtra. We coordinate venue deliveries directly across Pune, Pimpri-Chinchwad, and the greater Maharashtra region, with freight shipping available for destination events nationwide.
